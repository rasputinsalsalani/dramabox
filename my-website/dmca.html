1. How to Submit a Copyright Complaint

Please send a written notice to our contact email address. To help us review your request, include the following details:

  1. Your full name and contact information, including a valid email address.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed.
  3. The exact MovieFlixHD URL where the reported material can be accessed.
  4. A description of the material and its location on the page.
  5. An explanation of your ownership of the work or your authority to act on behalf of the copyright owner.
  6. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  7. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, where applicable, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the rights holder.
  8. Your physical or electronic signature.
